About the flock

Raised slow, on real pasture.

The flock moves across fresh grass every few days inside a mobile coop, so the birds are always on clean ground and the pasture gets to rest and recover. No cages, no crowding — just daylight, bugs, and room to range.

Every bird here is a heritage breed chosen for hardiness and flavor over speed. It takes longer to raise them this way. We think you can taste the difference.
